How I work

No two businesses are cut from the same stone, so I never start with a tool and go looking for somewhere to put it.

I start with how your team actually works: where the hours go, which jobs everyone dreads, and what the systems you already pay for could be doing.

Sometimes the answer is a brand-new AI product. Sometimes it’s forty lines of automation that hand someone their Friday back.

If AI isn’t the answer, I’ll tell you. If it is, I’ll build it properly.

What I build

Most projects use two or three of these layers. Some go all the way down.

  1. Sandstone

    AI agents

    Assistants for your customers and task-runners for your team. Agents that look things up, fill in the forms, chase the replies and hand over to a person when it matters.

  2. Shale

    Intelligent automation

    The copy-pasting, the chasing, the weekly report nobody reads until it’s late. I wire those jobs up so they run on their own, and your people get their afternoons back.

  3. Breccia

    Crypto and trading systems

    Bots, signal models and execution infrastructure for crypto and traditional markets. Backtested properly, wired to the exchanges you use, with risk limits that actually stop things.

  4. Limestone

    AI strategy and consultancy

    You know something has to change but not where to start. We sit down, map where the hours and the money go, and you leave with a plan you could hand to any developer. Ideally me.

  5. Conglomerate

    Digital product development

    From a sketch on a napkin to something people log into. Prototypes in days, real products in weeks, with the unglamorous parts (logins, payments, hosting) done properly.

  6. Dolomite

    Software integration

    Your CRM, your inbox, your spreadsheets and that one system from 2009. I connect them so data gets typed in once and lands everywhere it should.

  7. Granite

    Custom AI models

    When an off-the-shelf model starts guessing, I build one that knows. Trained around your data, your rules and your edge cases, and hosted where you want it.

Crypto and trading systems

Markets are where AI gets tested hardest. The data never stops, mistakes cost money in seconds, and a model that looked brilliant in a backtest can fall over on day one. It’s where I learned to build.

Trading bots
Automated strategies that run around the clock on the exchanges you use, with logs a person can actually read.
Signal and prediction models
Models that turn price, order book, on-chain and sentiment data into signals, tested out of sample before they go near real money.
Backtesting
Honest backtests with fees, slippage and funding included, so the numbers mean something.
Market data pipelines
Real-time feeds from exchanges and data providers, cleaned and stored so every model reads the same truth.
Risk controls
Position limits, kill switches and alerts that act faster than you can.
Dashboards
One screen for positions, profit and loss, and system health, on your desk or your phone.
