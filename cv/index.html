Profile

I have been building software with AI and machine learning since before it became fashionable. I started with prediction models and trading research, then moved on to building tools businesses rely on every day, from AML and KYC platforms and estimating tools for tradespeople, to products for the life sciences sector.

My strength is in the work before the code. I do my best work when I sit down with a business, find where it is losing time, money, or both, and build something practical to fix it. I build and ship quickly, keep costs down, and explain how things work in plain language, so people feel confident using what I build.

I am an independent contractor and I am available for my next engagement following the successful completion of several projects.

Key skills

  • AI and data: AI agents with tool calling, data extraction from documents, lead scoring, predictive modelling, backtesting and real-time market data feeds.
  • How I build: Claude Code, Cursor and the Claude API. I write specs and acceptance tests, then check AI outputs against real data before shipping.
  • Platforms and integrations: Vercel, AWS Lambda, Supabase, SQLite, GitHub, Stripe and Zoho.
  • Working with clients: scoping projects with business owners, giving fixed-price quotes, handing over in plain English and looking after systems once they are live.
